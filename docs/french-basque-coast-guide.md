## 🇫🇷 OVERVIEW: FRENCH BASQUE COUNTRY

**The Vibe:**
- Same Basque culture, French influence
- More Belle Époque elegance (19th century resort architecture)
- Surf culture meets French sophistication
- Less touristy than Spanish side (but Biarritz is popular)
- French food + Basque pintxos = excellent combo

**Main Towns:**
1. **Biarritz** — surf + sophistication, most touristy
2. **Bayonne** — cultural heart, chocolate, university town
3. **Saint-Jean-de-Luz** — charming fishing port, very Basque
4. **Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port** — mountain town, Camino starting point

**Easy from San Sebastián:** 30-45 min drive to any of these

---

## 🏄 BIARRITZ — Surf Capital & Belle Époque Resort

### **The Town**

**History:**
- 19th century became playground for European royalty
- Empress Eugénie (Napoleon III's wife) vacationed here
- Developed as elegant resort town
- 1950s-60s: American surfers discovered it = surf culture

**Today:**
- Mix of surf culture + old-world elegance
- Beautiful beaches
- Belle Époque architecture
- International surf competitions
- Young surfers + older French vacationers
- Digital nomad scene growing

### **Main Beaches**

**Grande Plage**
- Most famous, right in town center
- Backed by Casino and Grand Hotel
- Belle Époque buildings
- Good for swimming, beginner surf
- Gets crowded in summer

**Côte des Basques**
- South of town (15 min walk)
- **Best surf beach** in Biarritz
- More laid-back vibe
- Sunset spot
- Beach bars

**Marbella Beach**
- North of town
- Quieter, locals' beach
- Rock formations

**Plage de la Milady**
- South, more secluded
- Good surf
- Less crowded

### **What to Do**

**Surf:**
- Rent board at any beach (€15-25)
- Surf schools everywhere
- Consistent waves year-round
- June = smaller summer swell

**Rocher de la Vierge (Virgin Rock):**
- Iconic rock formation with statue
- Walkway extends into ocean
- Stunning views
- Great sunset spot

**Phare de Biarritz (Lighthouse):**
- 19th century lighthouse
- Climb for panoramic views
- Coastal walk from here

**Musée de la Mer (Sea Museum):**
- Aquarium with local marine life
- Basque maritime history
- Shark feeding shows

**Market:**
- Les Halles covered market
- Fresh seafood, local produce, cheese
- Morning visit, grab food for picnic

**Coastal Walk:**
- Follow coastline north or south
- Dramatic cliffs, beaches, views
- Can walk to Bidart (south) or Anglet (north)

### **Food Scene**

**Pintxos Bars (Basque tapas):**
- Bar Jean
- Chez Albert
- Mix of French + Basque cuisine

**French Bistros:**
- Le Clos Basque
- Chez Gladines

**Seafood:**
- Fresh fish at market restaurants
- Oysters, mussels, seafood platters

**Lunch on the Beach:**
- Beach clubs along Côte des Basques
- Casual, feet-in-sand dining

### **Nightlife**

**Young Scene:**
- Surfing community hangouts
- Beach bars at Côte des Basques
- Bar hopping in town center
- More international vibe than Spanish side

**Note:** Not as wild as San Sebastián, more chill

### **Where to Stay**

**Town Center:**
- Near Grande Plage
- Walking to everything
- More expensive (€100-150/night)

**Near Côte des Basques:**
- Surf neighborhood
- More laid-back
- Slightly cheaper (€80-120/night)

**Anglet (next town north):**
- Quieter, local vibe
- Good surf beaches
- Cheaper (€60-90/night)

## 🍫 BAYONNE — Cultural Heart of French Basque Country

### **The Town**

**Why It's Special:**
- **Real Basque culture** (not touristy resort)
- University town = young energy
- Confluence of two rivers (Nive & Adour)
- Historic old town
- Famous for chocolate (brought from New World via Bayonne)

**Vibe:**
- More authentic than Biarritz
- Locals actually live here
- Beautiful architecture
- Walkable old town
- Better food scene

### **What to Do**

**Old Town (Grand Bayonne & Petit Bayonne):**
- Wander narrow streets
- Half-timbered Basque houses
- River walks
- Very picturesque

**Cathédrale Sainte-Marie:**
- Gothic cathedral
- UNESCO site
- Beautiful cloisters

**Chocolate:**
- **Atelier du Chocolat** — chocolate museum + tasting
- **Cazenave** — historic chocolate shop (1854), hot chocolate
- **Daranatz** — traditional chocolatier
- Bayonne invented French chocolate-making

**Museums:**
- **Musée Basque** — best Basque culture museum
- Learn history, traditions, mythology
- Highly recommended

**Market:**
- Les Halles de Bayonne
- Fresh produce, cheese, charcuterie
- Saturday morning best

**River Walk:**
- Stroll along Nive River
- Cafes with terraces
- Very peaceful

### **Food Scene**

**Best Pintxos:**
- Bar du Marché
- Le Café du Théâtre
- More local, less touristy than Biarritz

**Traditional Basque:**
- Auberge du Cheval Blanc
- Le Bayonnais

**Jambon de Bayonne:**
- Famous cured ham (like prosciutto)
- Try at any charcuterie

### **Young People Scene**

**University Town:**
- Students everywhere
- Affordable bars
- Live music venues
- More local, less international tourists

**Best Neighborhoods:**
- Petit Bayonne (across river)
- Bar scene, younger vibe

### **Where to Stay**

**Old Town:**
- Most atmospheric
- Walking to everything
- €70-110/night

**Cheaper:**
- Slightly outside center
- €50-80/night

**Why Stay Here vs Biarritz:**
- More authentic
- Better food
- Cheaper
- Less touristy
- Easy day trip to Biarritz beaches (15 min)

## 🎣 SAINT-JEAN-DE-LUZ — Charming Fishing Port

### **The Town**

**Why It's Special:**
- Most "Basque" of the French towns
- Working fishing port
- Less touristy than Biarritz
- Beautiful bay
- Louis XIV married here (1660)

**Vibe:**
- Traditional, authentic
- Colorful houses
- Harbor with fishing boats
- Family-friendly
- Quieter than Biarritz

### **What to Do**

**Harbor:**
- Watch fishermen unload catch
- Colorful Basque boats
- Seafood restaurants on waterfront

**Beach (Grande Plage):**
- Protected bay = calm water
- Good for swimming
- Less surf than Biarritz
- Beautiful backdrop

**Old Town:**
- Pedestrian streets
- Basque architecture
- Rue Gambetta (main shopping street)

**Église Saint-Jean-Baptiste:**
- Where Louis XIV married
- Beautiful Basque-style church

**Maison de l'Infante:**
- Where queen stayed before wedding
- Can visit

**Ciboure:**
- Across harbor (connected by bridge)
- Even quieter
- Maurice Ravel birthplace

### **Food:**

**Fresh Seafood:**
- Directly from boats
- Grill restaurants on harbor
- Sardines, anchovies, tuna

**Macarons:**
- Maison Adam — invented here!
- Traditional recipe since 1660

**Pintxos:**
- Basque bars in old town
- More traditional than trendy

### **Where to Stay**

**Near Harbor:**
- Best atmosphere
- Walking to beach + restaurants
- €80-130/night

**Why Stay Here:**
- Most charming of the three
- Quieter than Biarritz
- Authentic fishing town
- Beautiful

## ⛰️ SAINT-JEAN-PIED-DE-PORT — Mountain Gateway

### **The Town**

**Why It's Special:**
- Medieval walled town in mountains
- **Starting point of Camino de Santiago** (French Way)
- Pilgrims gather here before crossing Pyrenees
- Beautiful mountain setting

**Vibe:**
- Spiritual (Camino pilgrims)
- Medieval architecture
- Mountain town atmosphere
- Lots of hiking

### **What to Do**

**Old Town:**
- Walk ramparts
- Citadelle (fortress)
- Views of Pyrenees
- Medieval streets

**Pilgrims:**
- Watch pilgrims start their Camino
- Albergues everywhere
- Pilgrim blessing at church (if interested)

**Hiking:**
- Pyrenees foothills
- Day hikes into mountains

**Camino Experience:**
- Stay in albergue if you want authentic pilgrim experience
- Could walk first stage of Camino (to Roncesvalles, Spain)
- 25km mountain crossing — challenging!

### **Where to Stay**

**In Town:**
- Hotels or albergues
- €40-90/night (hotels), €15-25 (albergues)

**Why Go Here:**
- Different vibe from coast
- Mountains, hiking
- Camino spirituality
- Medieval charm

**Distance from Coast:**
- 45 min from Bayonne
- 1 hour from Biarritz
- Makes good day trip OR overnight

## 🚗 DRIVING LOGISTICS

**Distances from San Sebastián:**
- Hondarribia (Spanish border town): 30 min
- Saint-Jean-de-Luz: 35 min
- Biarritz: 45 min
- Bayonne: 50 min
- Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port: 1h 15min
- Zugarramurdi Cave: 1 hour

**Border Crossing:**
- Literally just drive through
- No checkpoints, no passport control
- Schengen zone = seamless

**Parking:**
- Biarritz: paid lots (€2-3/hour)
- Bayonne: parking garages (€10-15/day)
- Saint-Jean-de-Luz: street parking or lots
- All manageable

**Tolls:**
- Some French highways have tolls (small, few euros)
- Have card or cash

## 🌊 FRENCH BASQUE VS SPANISH BASQUE

**Similarities:**
- Same culture, language (Euskara)
- Pintxos/tapas tradition
- Basque architecture
- Coastal beauty

**Differences:**

| Spanish Side | French Side |
|--------------|-------------|
| More lively nightlife | More refined, quieter |
| Cheaper | Slightly more expensive |
| More touristy (San Sebastián) | Less touristy (except Biarritz) |
| Dramatic cliffs | Gentler beaches |
| Pintxos culture dominates | French cuisine + Basque mix |
| Young backpacker scene | Surf + older French vacationers |
| Less English | More English (international surf scene) |

**Both:**
- Beautiful
- Great food
- Basque culture
- Surf opportunities
- Easy to explore
