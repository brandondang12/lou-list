## 🏔️ TOP PYRENEES DAY HIKES (1-2 Hours from San Sebastián)

### **#1: VALLE DE BAZTAN — La Rhune / Larrun Summit** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Distance from San Sebastián:** 45 min  
**Location:** French-Spanish border (Basque Pyrenees)  
**Elevation:** 905m (2,969 ft)  
**Hike:** 3-4 hours round trip (or take rack railway up!)  
**Difficulty:** Moderate  

**Why It's Perfect:**
- **Views:** 360° panorama — Atlantic Ocean + Pyrenees
- **Lush:** Green Basque mountains (not barren high peaks)
- **Accessible:** Can hike OR take historic rack railway (1924)
- **Basque sacred mountain** — spiritual significance

**The Hike:**
- Trailhead: Col de Saint-Ignace (French side)
- Gradual climb through pastures
- Wild ponies (pottok horses) grazing
- Summit has panorama + restaurant

**The Railway Option:**
- Take wooden rack train to summit (€11 round trip)
- Hike down (easier on knees)
- OR just ride for views + lunch

**Scenery:**
- Rolling green hills
- Ocean views on clear days
- Traditional Basque shepherds
- Very lush, not rocky

**Vibe:**
- Basque countryside meets Pyrenees
- Iconic mountain (you see it from San Sebastián on clear days)
- Historic train = fun experience

### **#2: SELVA DE IRATI (Irati Forest)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Distance from San Sebastián:** 1.5 hours  
**Location:** Navarre Pyrenees  
**Elevation:** 1,000-1,300m  
**Hike:** Multiple options (2-5 hours)  
**Difficulty:** Easy to moderate  

**Why It's Perfect:**
- **One of Europe's largest beech forests** — incredibly lush!
- **Fairy-tale atmosphere** — ancient trees, moss, streams
- **Not high-altitude** — forested valleys
- **Multiple trail options** — choose your length

**Recommended Hikes:**

**Option A: Reservoir Loop**
- Around Irabia Reservoir
- 3-4 hours
- Gentle, forest walking
- Lake views

**Option B: Peak of Ortzanzurieta**
- Forest + meadow views
- 4-5 hours
- More climbing but worth it
- Panoramic views of forest

**Scenery:**
- **VERY green** — beech forest is lush
- Mountain streams
- Meadows with wildflowers (June/July)
- Feels like you're in a fairytale

**Vibe:**
- Quiet, peaceful, mystical
- Less touristy than famous peaks
- More forest than rocky peaks
- Perfect for slow hiking

### **#3: RONCAL VALLEY — Larra-Belagua** 

**Distance from San Sebastián:** 1.5-2 hours  
**Location:** Eastern Navarre Pyrenees  
**Elevation:** 1,300-2,000m  
**Hike:** Multiple options  
**Difficulty:** Moderate to challenging  

**Why It's Great:**
- Dramatic karst landscapes (white limestone)
- Green valleys + high peaks combo
- Traditional Pyrenean villages
- Less crowded

**Recommended Hike:**
- **Mesa de los Tres Reyes** area trails
- High plateaus with views
- Mix of green valleys below + peaks above

**Scenery:**
- Mix of lush valleys + dramatic peaks
- Traditional shepherds' cabins
- Wildflowers in meadows

**Vibe:**
- More remote, authentic
- Serious Pyrenees feel
- Beautiful villages (Isaba, Roncal)

## 🏡 WHERE TO STAY (Pyrenees Vibe, Your Style)

### **#1: BAZTAN VALLEY — Small Village Stay** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Location:** Valle de Baztan (45 min from San Sebastián)  
**Towns:** Elizondo, Arizkun, Amaiur  
**Vibe:** Traditional Basque Pyrenees villages, green valleys, authentic

**Why It's Perfect for You:**
- **Close to La Rhune hike**
- Traditional Basque architecture
- Small villages (not touristy)
- Great food (Basque cuisine)
- Lush green valleys
- Easy access but feels remote

**Accommodation Options:**

**Elizondo (main town):**
- Casa rural (traditional guesthouse)
- Small hotels
- €60-100/night
- Town has restaurants, shops

**Amaiur/Arizkun (smaller):**
- More remote
- Casas rurales
- Very quiet, authentic
- €50-80/night

**What You'd Do:**
- Day 1: Arrive, explore village
- Day 2: Hike La Rhune
- Evening: Local restaurant, chill

**Distance to Next Stop:**
- Back to San Sebastián: 45 min
- To Biarritz: 1 hour

### **#2: IRATI FOREST AREA — Mountain Refuge or Village**

**Location:** Near Selva de Irati  
**Towns:** Ochagavía, Isaba  
**Vibe:** Mountain villages, serious Pyrenees, quiet

**Why It's Great:**
- Base for Irati Forest hikes
- Traditional stone villages
- More remote feeling
- Beautiful Pyrenean architecture

**Accommodation Options:**

**Ochagavía:**
- Medieval Pyrenean village
- Stone houses, river
- Small hotels, casas rurales
- €60-90/night
- Charming town square

**Mountain Refuges:**
- Casas de Irati (at forest edge)
- More rustic
- €40-70/night
- Right at trailheads

**What You'd Do:**
- Afternoon: Arrive Ochagavía, explore village
- Next day: Full day Irati forest hike
- Evening: Local restaurant (lamb, mountain cuisine)

**Distance:**
- San Sebastián: 1.5 hours
- Deeper in Pyrenees than Baztan

## 🌤️ JUNE/JULY WEATHER IN PYRENEES

**Basque Pyrenees (La Rhune, Baztan):**
- June/July: 65-75°F
- Green and lush
- Some afternoon rain possible
- Cool mornings

**Higher Pyrenees (Irati, Roncal):**
- Slightly cooler: 60-70°F
- More chance of afternoon clouds
- Very green

**Best for hiking:** Morning starts
