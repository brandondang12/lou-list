# Dirt Riding from Venice (No Car Needed)

**For:** Brandon's e-bike  
**Goal:** String together dirt sections without driving  
**Start:** Venice

## 🚴 THE VENICE → DIRT LOOP OPTIONS

### **Option 1: VENICE → BALLONA CREEK → KENNETH HAHN (Dirt)**

**Total Distance:** 12-15 miles round trip  
**Dirt Portion:** 3-5 miles  
**Time:** 1.5-2 hours  

**The Route:**

**Start: Venice**
- Head inland on Venice Blvd or Washington
- Pick up **Ballona Creek bike path** (paved)

**Ballona Creek Path:**
- 7 miles inland (paved but scenic)
- Takes you to Culver City area

**Turn-off to Kenneth Hahn:**
- Exit path near La Cienega
- Ride to **Kenneth Hahn State Recreation Area**
- Enter park (free for bikes)

**Dirt Section:**
- Park has dirt trails around lake
- Dirt paths on hillsides
- 3-5 miles of dirt options
- Rolling terrain, nothing too technical
- City views from top

**Return:**
- Same way back via Ballona Creek
- OR mix it up through Culver City streets

**Why It Works:**
- No car needed!
- Mix of paved commute + dirt riding
- Good workout
- Kenneth Hahn has actual dirt trails

---

### **Option 2: VENICE → BALDWIN HILLS SCENIC OVERLOOK (Stairs + Dirt)**

**Total Distance:** 10-12 miles round trip  
**Dirt Portion:** 2-3 miles  
**Time:** 1-1.5 hours  

**The Route:**

**Venice → Baldwin Hills:**
- Ride via Jefferson or Culver Blvd
- About 5 miles to Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook
- Mix of bike lanes and streets

**Baldwin Hills:**
- Park has dirt trails around the perimeter
- Some single-track
- Rolling hills
- Great views (downtown LA, ocean on clear days)

**The Dirt:**
- Trails around overlook base
- Connect to Kenneth Hahn trails
- Mix of dirt paths and fire roads
- Your e-bike handles it fine

**Return:**
- Ride back via streets
- Or connect through Culver City

**Why It Works:**
- Close (5 miles from Venice)
- Actual dirt trails
- Good views
- Urban adventure feel

### **Option 3: VENICE → SANTA MONICA MOUNTAINS (via Streets)**

**Total Distance:** 20-30 miles round trip  
**Dirt Portion:** 5-10 miles  
**Time:** 2-3 hours  

**The Route:**

**Venice → Temescal Canyon:**
- Ride up Sunset Blvd or PCH + side streets
- ~8-10 miles to Temescal Canyon
- Mix of bike lanes

**Temescal Gateway Park:**
- Pay $12 parking (or free for bikes?)
- Access dirt trails and fire roads
- 5-10 miles of dirt options
- This is real mountain dirt

**The Dirt:**
- Temescal Ridge fire road
- Canyon trails
- Rolling to climbing terrain
- Your e-bike shines on climbs

**Return:**
- Ride back down to coast
- PCH or residential streets

**Why It Works:**
- Real mountain trails
- No car (just longer ride to get there)
- Ocean views
- Legit dirt experience

**Caveat:**
- Longer commitment (20-30 miles total)
- More climbing
- E-bike battery management important

## 🔋 E-BIKE BATTERY STRATEGY

**For 10-12 mile rides (Kenneth Hahn, Baldwin Hills):**
- Start in Eco mode
- Should have plenty of battery
- No worries

**For 18-30 mile rides (Sullivan, Temescal):**
- **Paved sections:** Eco or Off (conserve battery)
- **Climbs:** Tour or Sport (use assist)
- **Dirt descents:** Off (let gravity work)
- **Range check:** Most e-bikes = 20-40 miles mixed terrain

**Battery Tips:**
- Charge fully before leaving
- Check battery % at turnaround (need enough to get back)
- Eco mode on flats/slight downhills
- Save power for climbs
- Worst case: turn off assist, pedal home (good workout!)

## 💡 STRINGING TOGETHER DIRT

**The Reality:**
- Venice is beach/urban = limited immediate dirt
- **Best close dirt:** Kenneth Hahn (7 miles away)
- **Best dirt worth riding to:** Sullivan Canyon (9 miles away)

**You CAN do no-car dirt missions:**
- Just requires longer rides
- E-bike makes it totally doable
- Think of it as: adventure ride TO the dirt, then dirt riding, then ride home

**Compare to driving:**
- **Drive to Sullivan:** 15 min car, 1 hour dirt, 15 min back = 1.5 hours total
- **Ride to Sullivan:** 30 min ride, 1 hour dirt, 30 min back = 2 hours total
- Not that different! Plus more exercise.
