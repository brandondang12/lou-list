## 🏔️ TOP RECOMMENDATIONS

### **1. SULLIVAN CANYON FIRE ROAD** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Distance from Venice:** 15-20 min  
**Location:** Brentwood/Pacific Palisades  
**Surface:** Wide fire road, packed dirt  
**Length:** 5-10 miles (out and back, you choose distance)  
**Difficulty:** Easy to moderate  

**Why It's Perfect:**
- Super close to Venice
- Beautiful canyon scenery
- Mostly flat to gentle grades
- Wide fire road (safe, easy)
- Shaded in parts
- Popular but not crowded on weekends

**The Route:**
- Park at Queensfellow Dr trailhead (free street parking)
- Ride up Sullivan Canyon
- Connects to Westridge Fire Road if you want longer
- Turn around whenever

**Scenery:**
- Oak trees, sycamores
- Creek (seasonal)
- Views of canyon walls
- Peaceful, quiet

**E-Bike Notes:**
- Perfect for e-bike
- Some gentle climbs (e-bike makes easy)
- Wide enough to pass hikers safely
- Can go as far as you want

**Weekend Vibe:**
- Morning best (cooler, fewer people)
- Dog walkers, runners, cyclists
- Very local Venice/Westside crowd

---

### **2. TEMESCAL RIDGE TRAIL / FIRE ROAD**

**Distance from Venice:** 20-25 min  
**Location:** Pacific Palisades (Temescal Gateway Park)  
**Surface:** Fire road, some single-track options  
**Length:** 3-8 miles round trip  
**Difficulty:** Moderate (some climbing)  

**Why It's Great:**
- Ocean views!
- Multiple route options
- Connects to bigger trail network
- Good workout

**The Route:**
- Park at Temescal Gateway Park ($12 parking)
- Start on Temescal Ridge Trail
- Fire road to ridge
- Ocean views at top
- Can connect to Backbone Trail (longer option)

**Scenery:**
- Coastal views
- Chaparral
- Santa Monica Bay panorama
- On clear days: Malibu to Palos Verdes

**E-Bike Notes:**
- Climbing made easier with e-bike
- Fire road wide and smooth
- Some rockier sections (your tires can handle)
- Avoid technical single-track spurs

---

### **3. BALLONA CREEK BIKE PATH**

**Distance from Venice:** 5 min (starts IN Venice!)  
**Location:** Venice to Culver City  
**Surface:** Paved bike path  
**Length:** 7 miles one-way (14 round trip)  
**Difficulty:** Easy (flat)  

**Why Include It:**
- Right in your backyard
- Completely flat
- Safe, separated path
- Good for cruising/commuting

**The Route:**
- Starts at Venice Beach
- Follows Ballona Creek inland
- Ends at Syd Kronenthal Park (Culver City)
- Can extend to other paths

**Scenery:**
- Urban creek
- Wetlands restoration areas
- Not super scenic but functional

**E-Bike Notes:**
- Perfect for e-bike cruising
- Fast, flat, easy
- Good for morning coffee rides

**Weekend Vibe:**
- Lots of cyclists, families
- Safe, well-used

**Location:** Venice Beach to Torrance

**Length:** 22 miles one-way

### **6. TOPANGA STATE PARK (FIRE ROADS)**

**Distance from Venice:** 30-35 min  
**Location:** Topanga/Pacific Palisades  
**Surface:** Fire roads (avoid single-track)  
**Length:** Varies (5-15 miles)  
**Difficulty:** Moderate to challenging (elevation)  

**Why It's Great:**
- Largest state park in LA
- Extensive fire road network
- Incredible views
- True mountain experience

**Recommended Routes:**
- **Eagle Rock Loop** via fire roads (not technical single-track)
- **Temescal Ridge to Backbone** (fire road)
- **Los Liones to East Topanga** (wider trails)

**The Trails:**
- Park at Trippet Ranch or Los Liones
- Stick to fire roads
- Avoid rocky single-track

**Scenery:**
- 360° views
- Ocean + mountains
- Wildflowers (spring)
- Oak woodlands

**E-Bike Notes:**
- Fire roads are perfect
- Climbing is real (e-bike shines here)
- Check which trails allow bikes (some don't)
- Avoid East Topanga Fire Road on weekends (cars allowed)

**Parking:**
- $10-12 day use fee
- Popular on weekends (go early)

## 🛠️ E-BIKE TIPS FOR TRAILS

**Battery Management:**
- Start in lower assist (save battery)
- Use higher assist for climbs
- Range: 20-40 miles depending on assist level

**Trail Etiquette:**
- Yield to hikers
- Announce when passing ("On your left!")
- Keep moderate speed on shared trails
- Smile, be friendly

**What to Bring:**
- Water (2 liters)
- Snacks
- Phone (GPS, emergency)
- Basic tool kit
- Tire pump
- First aid (small)

**Check Before You Go:**
- Battery charged
- Tire pressure
- Brakes working
- Trail conditions (after rain, some close)

**E-Bike Rules:**
- Class 1 e-bikes (pedal-assist only) allowed on most trails
- Class 2/3 (throttle) may be restricted
- Check park regulations
- Some single-track prohibits bikes (stick to fire roads)

---

## 📅 WEEKEND LOGISTICS

**Best Times:**
- **Saturday/Sunday mornings: 7-9am** (cool, fewer people)
- **Weekday afternoons** (if you can swing it — much less crowded)

**Avoid:**
- Mid-day weekends (hot, crowded)
- After 10am on popular trails

**Parking:**
- Get there early (popular trailheads fill up)
- Some require day-use fees ($10-12)
- Street parking often free (Sullivan Canyon, etc.)

**Weather:**
- Check wind (coastal can be strong)
- Summer: early morning best (heat)
- Winter: mid-day warmer
- After rain: trails may be muddy (avoid)
