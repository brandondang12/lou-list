## 🌊 WHAT IS "FLOW"?

**Flow trails have:**
- ✅ Smooth, swoopy descents
- ✅ Banked turns (berms)
- ✅ Rollers and small jumps (you can roll over)
- ✅ Momentum-based riding
- ✅ Fun downhills (not just grinding climbs)

**Your e-bike can handle:**
- Flow-style single-track (not too rocky)
- Smooth dirt with good tread
- Moderate technical features
- NOT: super rocky, root-filled, or extreme drops

## 🏆 TOP FLOW TRAILS

### **#1: DIRT MULHOLLAND → HUB JUNCTION** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Distance from Venice:** 25-30 min  
**Location:** Santa Monica Mountains (off Mulholland)  
**Surface:** Single-track with FLOW  
**Length:** 5-8 miles (various loops)  
**Difficulty:** Moderate (some technical bits, mostly flow)  

**Why It's THE Flow Trail:**
- **Classic LA flow descent**
- Berms, rollers, smooth turns
- Fast, fun, swoopy
- This is what you're looking for!

**The Route:**
- Park at Dirt Mulholland Trailhead (Mulholland Dr near Encino Hills Dr)
- **Option 1:** Ride up fire road (e-bike makes easy), descend Dirt Mulholland (FLOW!)
- **Option 2:** Hub Junction loop (mix of climb + flow descent)
- **Option 3:** Out-and-back on Dirt Mulholland section

**The Flow:**
- Smooth hardpack dirt
- Banked corners
- Rolling terrain
- Speed-friendly
- Some rocky sections (your tires handle it)

**E-Bike Notes:**
- Class 1 allowed
- Climb up fire roads (e-assist shines)
- Descend flow sections (turn off assist, enjoy!)
- Popular with MTB crowd (be courteous)

**What to Know:**
- Weekends get busy (go early, 7-8am)
- Some technical rocky sections (walk if needed)
- Multiple route options (use AllTrails/Trailforks)
- Parking fills up

**Post-Ride:**
- Coffee in Woodland Hills or back to Venice

**THIS IS IT** — if you want flow, start here.

## 🚴 FLOW TRAIL RIDING TIPS

**E-Bike Strategy:**
- **Climb:** Use assist (save legs)
- **Flow descent:** Turn assist LOW or OFF
  - Let gravity + momentum do the work
  - Feel the flow
  - Assist can make you too fast (less control)

**Technique:**
- **Look ahead** (not at your wheel)
- **Weight back** on descents
- **Lean into berms** (trust them)
- **Pump the rollers** (up/down body motion for speed)
- **Smooth is fast** (don't brake mid-turn)

**Safety:**
- Start slow (learn the trail)
- Speed up as you learn lines
- Walk technical sections if unsure
- Wear helmet + gloves

**Etiquette:**
- Yield to uphill riders
- Call out when passing ("On your left!")
- Don't skid (ruins trail)
- Stay on trail (don't cut corners)

## 🛠️ E-BIKE SETUP FOR FLOW

**Before You Go:**
- **Tire pressure:** 25-30 PSI (lower = more grip, cushion)
- **Suspension:** Set sag (25-30% compression when you sit)
- **Brakes:** Check they're working (you'll need them!)
- **Battery:** Full charge (assist on climbs, not descents)

**Settings:**
- **Eco/Low** assist for climbs (save battery)
- **Off or Eco** for descents (feel the flow)
- **Tour/Mid** if you want assist on flats

**Safety Check:**
- Helmet (non-negotiable)
- Gloves (grip + protection)
- Knee pads (optional but nice)
- Eye protection (dust, bugs)

## ⚠️ E-BIKE TRAIL ACCESS

**Important:**
- **Class 1 e-bikes** (pedal-assist only, max 20mph assist) = allowed on most trails
- **Class 2/3** (throttle, 28mph) = often restricted
- **Check each trail** — some don't allow any e-bikes
- **Be respectful** — MTB community has mixed feelings about e-bikes

**Where E-Bikes Are Welcome:**
- Most fire roads (definitely)
- Many single-track trails (check signs)
- Purpose-built flow trails (often yes)

**Where They're Not:**
- Some narrow single-track
- Wilderness areas
- Posted "no e-bike" trails

**Check:**
- Trailhead signs
- Park websites
- Call ranger if unsure
